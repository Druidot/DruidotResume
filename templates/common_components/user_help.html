🛠️ Resume Processing Help Guide

1 Search & Select a Company

From the dashboard, use the search bar or click on a company name to view its details.

Example: Click on "TCS", "Infosys", etc. to proceed.

2 Select Filter Criteria

Once on the company page, choose from the following filters to narrow down resumes:

  • 📍 Branch
  • 🏙️ City
  • 🏢 Department
  • 📁 Project Name

3 Choose an Action

After selecting filters, choose one of the following:

Option 1: Add New Job Description (JD)
  • Click the "Add JD" button.
  • Fill in job title, skills, responsibilities, etc.
  • Submit to save the JD for the selected company and criteria.
Option 2: View Resumes
  • Click the "View Resumes" button.
  • System will fetch all resumes matching the selected filters.
  • You can then view, select, or reject resumes.

4 Review & Process Resumes

View detailed resume cards with name, skills, experience, etc.

  • ✅ Select candidates
  • ❌ Reject candidates
  • 💾 Download resumes as PDF

Contact your admin or support team for more advanced setup or integration support.
