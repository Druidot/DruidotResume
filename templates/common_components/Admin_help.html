🧾 Help Guide - Company Registration

Use the Company Registration tab to add your company's details to the system. Please ensure all fields are filled correctly before submitting.

📋 Fields Explained

  • Company Name: Enter the official name of your company (e.g., Acme Corporation).
  • Address: Include street, city, and postal code (e.g., 123 Main St, Springfield).
  • Email: Must be a valid email address (e.g., contact@acme.com).
  • Phone: Use a valid phone format including area code (e.g., +1-800-555-1234).

✅ How to Submit

After filling all fields, click the "Register" button. A success message will confirm that your company was registered.

📌 Notes

  • Fields marked with * are mandatory.
  • Email and Phone must be in correct format to avoid validation errors.
  • Make sure there are no typos in Company Name or Address.

🧾 Help Guide - User Registration & Management

This section explains how to manage users in the system. You can register new users, verify accounts, activate them, and assign roles such as Admin, User, or Customer.

📋 Fields Explained

  • Username: Unique identifier for login and account reference (e.g., john_doe).
  • Email: Must be a valid and unique email address (e.g., john@example.com).
  • Password: Choose a strong password; it is stored securely in the system.
  • Verified: Indicates whether the user's email or phone has been verified.
  • Active: Only active users can log in or use the system features.
  • Role: Assign one of the roles: Admin, User, or Customer.

👤 User Roles

  • Admin: Has full access to all features and can manage other users and system settings.
  • User: Has limited access, typically internal or staff members with restricted permissions.
  • Customer: End-user of the system, usually the ones registering companies or branches.

✅ How to Register and Activate

Fill in all the required fields on the User Registration form. After submission:

  • An email/phone verification process may be triggered.
  • Once verified, mark the account as active.
  • Assign an appropriate role depending on the user's responsibilities.

📌 Notes

  • Fields marked with * are mandatory.
  • Verified and active status must be True for a user to successfully log in.
  • Be careful while assigning roles; Admin has complete system control.
  • Use secure password practices and encourage users to update their passwords regularly.

🔓 How to Logout

To securely logout from your account, follow these steps:

  • Click on your Profile Photo or User Icon in the top-right corner of the page.
  • A dropdown menu will appear.
  • Click the "Logout" or "Sign Out" button
  • You will be safely redirected to the login screen.

👤 How to Add or Modify Profile Details

You can easily update your profile details including your photo, contact information, and more. Follow the steps below:

  • Click on your Profile Icon in the top-right corner of the page.
  • Select the option "User Profile" from the dropdown menu.
  • A popup window (or modal) will appear showing your current profile information.
  • Update the fields such as:
    • Full Name
    • Email Address
    • Phone Number
    • Profile Photo (upload a new picture)
    • Address, etc.
  • After making changes, click the "Save" or "Update" button at the bottom of the popup.
  • A success message will confirm that your profile was updated.
